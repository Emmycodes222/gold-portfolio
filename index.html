Lumina

Lumina is a conceptual climate tech platform that enables neighborhoods to generate, store, and share renewable energy through a decentralixed micro-grid network. through a narrative driven illustration system, the project demonstrates how communities can remain powered by sharing renewable energy, even during power outages, while also reducing their carbon footprint and contributing to a more sustainable future.
